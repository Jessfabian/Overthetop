State review reference Important facts View facts
  1. The Trust Certificate F6734 is used in all states unless a state-specific version is indicated.
  2. The Absolute Assignment P4051 for an external 1035 exchange and Internal Surrender F6309 are used in all states.
  3. F7004 is used in all states unless a state-specific version is indicated.
  4. Backdating may be restricted by state and product type.
  5. Do not substitute a generic form when the state requires a state-specific version.
  6. Part 2 applications may have different versions. Confirm the correct version for the case.
  7. New York cases require additional illustration and replacement review.
Review process Form review procedure View procedure
  1. Confirm that the complete form was submitted.

    Review every page. A signature page by itself should not be treated as the complete form.

  2. Confirm the form version.

    When multiple versions exist, use the most recent accepted version unless an approved transition period applies.

  3. Review state-specific requirements.

    Select the contract state below to display forms, notices, replacement guidance and state-specific review notes.
